Over my years as a student and researcher, I have read and benefited from advice shared by senior scientists. Below are some resources that I have found particularly valuable and would like to share with students, especially those early in their careers.

## Advice on Learning and Studying Physics

- **[Enjoy Your Work](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/career-advice/enjoy-your-work/)** by Terence Tao
- **[One Thing You Must Understand About Studying Physics](http://jakobschwichtenberg.com/one-thing/)** by Jakob Schwichtenberg
- **[How to Learn Maths and Physics](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/books.html)** by John Baez
- **[How I Learned to Learn Physics](http://jakobschwichtenberg.com/level-3/)** by Jakob Schwichtenberg
- **[How to become a GOOD Theoretical Physicist](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~gadda001/goodtheorist/)** by Gerard 't Hooft
- **[Advice for the Young Scientist](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/advice.html)** by John Baez

## Research and Scientific Thinking

- **[You and Your Research](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/YouAndYourResearch.html)** by Richard Hamming
- **[Unfortunately, Repetition is a Convincing Argument](http://jakobschwichtenberg.com/unfortunately-repetition-is-a-convincing-argument/)** by Jakob Schwichtenberg
- **[Write Down What You Learn](http://jakobschwichtenberg.com/write-down-what-you-learn-or-why-i-wrote-a-textbook-for-students-as-a-student/)** by Jakob Schwichtenberg
- **[Writing tips](http://starboardshuttle.com/index.php/2021/03/30/students-writing-tips/)** by Lidia del Rio
- **[How to Teach Stuff](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/teaching.html)** by John Baez
- **[Four Golden Lessons](https://www.nature.com/articles/426389a)** by Steven Weinberg
- **[The Importance of Stupidity in Scientific Research](https://journals.biologists.com/jcs/article/121/11/1771/30038/The-importance-of-stupidity-in-scientific-research)** by Martin Schwartz
- **[Academic Agonies and How to Avoid Them](https://social-epistemology.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/joseph_agassi_academic-_agonies_and-_how_-to_avoid_them_serrc_2020.pdf)** by Joseph Agassi

## Communication and Teaching

- **[Tips for Giving a Great Talk](https://pirsa.org/22100109)** by Robert Spekkens
- **[Can French Administration be More Sexy than Quantum Gravity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=baFuLhTfiq8&ab_channel=TheQuantumInformationStructureofSpacetime)** by Pierre Martin-Dussaud
- **[Suggestions for Giving Talks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9703019.pdf)** by Robert Geroch
- **[Philosophy of Physics](https://www.hps.cam.ac.uk/students/research-guide/philosophy-physics)** (Research Guide) by Jeremy Butterfield
- **[Writing Philosophy](https://www.hps.cam.ac.uk/students/research-guide/writing-philosophy)** by Peter Lipton
- **[5 mins with Bob Coecke](https://www.xpanse.world/insight/quantum-in-pictures-quantum-guitar-music-from-qubits)**

## Blogs I Like

- [Logic Matters](https://www.logicmatters.net/blog/)
- [John Carlos Baez's Blog](https://johncarlosbaez.wordpress.com/)
- [Terence Tao's Blog](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/)
- [Peter Woit's Blog](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~woit/wordpress/)


These resources span various career stages and offer practical guidance on learning, conducting research, communicating ideas, and navigating academic life. I encourage students to engage with these materials and find the advice that resonates most with their own journey.
