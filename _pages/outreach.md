Over the past decade, I have been actively involved in science outreach and engagement, with a focus on making quantum physics accessible through visual and compositional approaches. Below are the key events, programs, and presentations.

## Prominent Events and Programs

### [Quantum in Pictures](https://www.quantinuum.com/news/quantum-in-pictures) (2022–present)
A comprehensive program teaching quantum physics to school students through diagrams and visual methods. Now active in more than twelve countries and adopted into Greece's national curriculum, with peer-reviewed publications documenting its effectiveness.

### [Physics in Action](https://educationinaction.org.uk/study-day/physics-in-action-6-12-2023/) (2023)
Royal Institution engagement with masterclass on quantum physics using pictorial methods.

### [Royal Institution Masterclasses](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/CSRIMasterclasses/) (2023)
Quantum in Pictures masterclass delivered through the Royal Institution's prestigious program.

### [Pint of Science Festival](https://pintofscience.co.uk/event/quantum-in-pictures-oxford) (2023)
Public engagement event communicating complex quantum concepts in accessible settings.

### [Marconi Day](https://www.hsm.ox.ac.uk/event/marconi-day-2023) (2023)
Scientific presentation and celebration of Marconi's contributions.

### [Marconi Van](https://marconivan.org/) (2021)
Mobile science outreach bringing quantum physics education to multiple communities.

### [UNIQ+](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/access/uniq-plus) (2021)
University of Oxford summer school engagement program.

### [UNIQ](https://www.uniq.ox.ac.uk/) (2021)
Oxford's summer school program for prospective students.

### [Physics Lab to Life](https://www2.physics.ox.ac.uk/events/2021/03/11/physics-lab-to-life-2021) (2021)
Practical physics laboratory engagement program.

### [Oxford Maths Festival](https://mathsfest.web.ox.ac.uk/home) (2020)
Community festival promoting mathematics and quantum concepts.

### [Marie Curious](https://www2.physics.ox.ac.uk/events/2020/02/15/marie-curious-2020) (2020)
Science communication and outreach initiative.

### [Stargazing Oxford](https://www2.physics.ox.ac.uk/events/2020/01/25/stargazing-oxford-2020) (2020, 2021)
Public astronomy and physics engagement events.

### [IF Oxford Science + Ideas Festival](https://if-oxford.com/) (2019)
Oxford's interdisciplinary festival of ideas and science.

### [Lahore Science Mela](http://ksslsm.org/) (2017, 2018, 2019)
Pakistan's largest annual science festival – I helped organize from its first edition and participated in multiple years.

### [STEM Activities at Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital](https://www.theiet.org/membership/member-news/member-news-2019/iet-member-news-q1-2019/volunteers-bring-stem-activities-to-hospital-wards/), Lahore (2018)
Outreach bringing science education to hospital communities.

### [Lab in the Classroom – Government Primary School](https://blogs.plos.org/thestudentblog/2019/09/20/science-outreach-the-catharsis-of-an-undergraduate/), G.T. Road, Lahore (2017–18)
Bringing laboratory experiences to primary school students.

### [Spectra Internship Program](https://spectramagazine.org/spectra-internship-program/) (2017, 2018, 2019, 2020)
A student-run science-writing platform I co-founded, providing mentorship and publication opportunities.
